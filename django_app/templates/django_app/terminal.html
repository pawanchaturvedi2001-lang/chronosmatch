ChronosMatch • 5-Minute Mentor Demonstration Guide

Welcome to the ChronosMatch Week 2 Project Review. Follow these five steps to demonstrate the full zero-copy architecture, Cython matching engine, and live order book execution to your mentor:

Step-by-Step Demonstration Sequence

  1. Start the Market Firehose: Locate Market Simulator Controls on the right. Leave default at 5,000 orders @ 25,000 ops/s and click START FIREHOSE. Observe mock orders stream through the shared-memory RingBuffer into the matching engine.
  2. Inspect Live Level 2 Depth & Spreads: Watch the Live Order Book update in real time with side-by-side BUY bids (green) and SELL asks (red). Note the real-time spread calculation and dynamic volume depth visualizer bars.
  3. Submit a Manual Cross Order: Under Order Entry Panel, click on any resting price in the book (or enter your own). Click BUY (Bid) or SELL (Ask) and press SUBMIT. Confirm the order crosses the book and emits instant trade fills in the Recent Executed Trades tape.
  4. Analyze Microsecond Latency Metrics: Review the Engine & Pipeline Latency Profile. Highlight the Mean (sub-2 µs), P95, and P99 tail latencies achieved by the statically typed Cython matching engine.
  5. Verify Verified System Health Diagnostics: Scroll down to System Health. Show that all core components (Django REST API, Native Cython `.pyd` module, mmap RingBuffer, and FIFO Order Book) report verified operational states.

Key Architectural Highlights to Mention

  • Zero-Copy IPC: 29-byte packed C structures (<QdIBQ) passed through memory-mapped buffers with atomic head/tail pointers.
  • Cython Native Hot-Path: Strict Price-Time priority matching accelerated with statically typed C integers and floats, using pure C nogil math routines.
  • No Hardcoded Stats: All metrics, depth levels, and trade logs are queried directly from the running matching engine instance.
